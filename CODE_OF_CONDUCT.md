# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the WayFind project welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus discussion on the work.
- Respect privacy. Never post real child, family, reporter, or case data, images, or identifying details in public project spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, threats, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Deliberately exposing credentials or sensitive case information is also unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may close or restrict participation in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. To report a concern, contact the repository owner through GitHub or use GitHub's reporting tools. For an immediate safety emergency, contact local emergency services; GitHub is not an emergency-response channel.

This Code of Conduct applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing the project.
